Different Texture Paints for Walls and How to Apply Them

PS
Pure Services Experts
• Updated on June 10, 2026 • Read Time: 8 mins

Plain walls are a thing of the past. Modern luxury interior design demands depth, character, and a premium tactile feel. Texture paints are the perfect solution to transform ordinary walls into stunning masterpieces. Whether you want a rustic metallic sheen, a sophisticated marble finish, or a bold geometric pattern, choosing the right texture paint can redefine your living space entirely.

Popular Types of Wall Texture Paints

Different textures require specific materials and specialized tools. Here are the top luxury finishes that are currently trending in high-end homes:

1. Metallic Royale Texture Finish

Perfect for focal or accent walls in the master bedroom or living room. It reflects light beautifully, giving the room a luminous and expansive luxury feel. Available in gold, bronze, copper, and silver undertones.

2. Stucco and Venetian Marble Texture

Inspired by classic Italian plastering, this finish mimics the look and gloss of high-end natural marble and polished stone. It is highly durable and brings an unmatched old-world charm combined with contemporary sleekness.

Step-by-Step Application Guide

Applying luxury texture finishes requires precision, patience, and professional tools like trowels, special rollers, and spatulas. Here is the absolute correct method to achieve a flawless finish:

1

Surface Preparation

Ensure the wall is completely clean, dry, and free from moisture, cracks, or loose paint particles. Sand the wall with emery paper for a smooth base.

2

Applying Wall Primer

Apply a coat of premium quality undercoat or wall primer. This allows the texture base to bond tightly with the masonry surface underneath.

3

Texture Base Application

Using a steel trowel or specialized application roller, apply the first thick base coat of the texture pattern evenly across the section.

4

Creating Patterns & Top Coat

While the paint is wet, manipulate it with stencils, sponges, or standard texturing tools to shape the final look. Let it dry completely for 24 hours before applying protective topcoats.

Pro-Tip for Homeowners

Unlike basic plastic paints, artistic texture work requires consistent hand-pressure and expert stroke angles. A small mistake can break the symmetry of the whole wall. It is always recommended to hire trained professionals to ensure absolute pixel-perfect results.
